# OP-002B Business Publication Capability

This bounded capability consumes an OP-002A `APPROVED` outcome and its canonical OP-001D/OP-001E associations, validates publication governance, records exactly one `PUBLISHED` or `REJECTED` outcome after `ELIGIBLE`, associates the outcome with Operational Status, and records audit evidence.

Execution stops after publication. No search, discovery, ranking, recommendations, marketplace, reviews, messaging, payments, notifications, workflow or runtime orchestration, AI publication, persistence, API, UI, database, or network behavior exists here.
