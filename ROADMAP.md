# Roadmap

## Mission 000B

Rebuild the official Khedmah Digital V1 repository foundation and restore the documentation source of truth.

## V1 Direction

V1 remains focused on an Arabic-first business growth platform foundation. Implementation work must follow approved scope and domain contracts.

## Current MVP Cycle

The council directive dated 2026-07-27 adopts the bounded [Khedmah Digital MVP Definition](docs/product/KHEDMAH-DIGITAL-MVP-DEFINITION.md). Current work is limited to closing its acceptance criteria, P0 security/data/authority gaps, and Alpha release gates.

Alpha is not approved while critical/high production dependency findings, migration authority, required persistence, central CI evidence, or other blocking gates remain open.

## Scope Protection

Future modules are documented as reserved until explicitly approved. Mission sequencing must not introduce backend, frontend, API, database, or infrastructure implementation outside the approved MVP definition or ahead of a later authorization.
