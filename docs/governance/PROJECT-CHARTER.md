# Project Charter

## Mission

Establish Khedmah Digital V1 as a controlled, Arabic-first business growth platform foundation.

## Repository Role

The repository is the permanent source of truth for approved governance, product, architecture, domain, operations, and vision documentation.

## V1 Success Criteria

- Scope is clear and protected.
- Product direction is Arabic-first.
- Future modules are reserved rather than implemented.
- Implementation begins only after approved documentation and mission authorization.

## Current MVP Authorization

The council directive dated 2026-07-27 approves the bounded [Khedmah Digital MVP Definition](../product/KHEDMAH-DIGITAL-MVP-DEFINITION.md). This authorization follows the documentation-foundation prerequisite and does not authorize excluded or deferred capabilities.

Alpha and production readiness remain separate gate decisions. Scope approval alone is not a release approval.
