## 2A. Anonymous tender privacy check — bounded and non-sensitive
- Sampled **16 published public tender cards** through `/_api/public/tenders` and the matching **16 details** through `/_api/public/tender?slug=...` on the Floot editor sandbox. Both endpoint families returned HTTP 200. Repeated only a bounded anonymous public read; did not retrieve or expose private customer/owner data.
- `title`, `issuingEntity`, `sector`, `location`, submission method and requirements are passed through `safePublicText`. The sample visibly redacted contact-like content in **5 list titles**, **5 detail titles**, and **4 submission-method fields**. No unredacted email or phone-like sequence was confirmed in the text fields whose purpose is free-form contact disclosure.
- A broad numeric regex flagged **5 reference numbers**, but the masked-format review showed **mixed alphanumeric procurement reference IDs** (not recognizable Syrian mobile numbers), illustrating why generic digit heuristics can produce false positives. Do **not** classify them as exposed telephone numbers or automatically erase tender reference numbers without stricter semantics and evidence.
- Limits: the sample does not guarantee every live tender has been checked, nor that all forms of address/contact obfuscation are covered; no logged-in recipient or authorization matrix was tested. Recheck public-tender text privacy as a separate release acceptance gate.
