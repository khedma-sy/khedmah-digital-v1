## 2. Platform-Owned Organization Concept

Official concept chain:

```text
Khedmah Digital Platform
↓
Platform-Owned Organization
↓
Official Business Profile
↓
Services
```

### Concept Definitions

| Concept | Meaning | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform identity | The product, brand, governance owner, and operating platform known as Khedmah Digital / خدمة ديجتل. | Not a normal provider competing for hidden ranking preference. |
| Platform-owned organization | The future organization record owned and managed by the platform itself. | Exists to represent the platform officially, not to bypass ordinary trust rules. |
| Official business profile | The public profile for Khedmah Digital services and public information. | Must follow public profile, discovery, trust, and sharing contracts. |
| Normal business profile | A provider-owned business such as restaurant, shop, workshop, salon, or service company. | Must not receive weaker verification just because the platform has an official profile. |
| Partner profile | A future partner ecosystem role that supports regional, digital, business, or community growth. | Not the same as the platform-owned organization and not an affiliate/commission seller. |
| Supplier profile | A future supplier/manufacturing/wholesale profile. | Not the same as Khedmah Digital itself and not a transaction marketplace listing. |

### Architecture Rule

Khedmah Digital may have an official profile, but that profile must be explicitly typed as `Platform-Owned Organization` so future systems can distinguish platform identity from ordinary businesses, partners, suppliers, representatives, and professionals.

## 5. Verification & Trust Contract

Future verification label: **Official Platform Verification**.

This verification means:

- Official platform entity.
- Ownership confirmed.
- Managed by platform.
- Public identity is controlled by Khedmah Digital governance.

This verification does not mean:

- Paid badge.
- Higher ranking.
- Marketplace approval.
- Advertising entitlement.
- Preferential discovery placement.
- Immunity from audit or governance.

### Trust Abuse Prevention

The official profile must prevent:

- Fake ratings.
- Artificial ranking.
- Unfair discovery advantage.
- Hidden promotion.
- Self-awarded trust level inflation.
- Use of platform ownership to suppress other businesses.

Trust must attach to the platform-owned organization as official-entity verification, while ordinary provider trust remains governed by the same Trust Foundation principles.

## 6. Discovery Neutrality Rules

Khedmah Digital may appear in discovery only through the same transparent discovery principles used for other public entities.

Discovery must prevent:

- Automatic top ranking.
- Paid visibility.
- Hidden preference.
- Exclusion of other businesses.
- Competitor suppression.
- Manipulation of category or location results.

Neutral discovery principles:

- If the profile matches a user search/category/location need, it may appear as a normal eligible result.
- Its official status may be displayed as trust information, not ranking logic.
- Discovery ordering must not favor platform-owned entities unless a future public governance rule applies equally to all entities.
- Any sponsored, promoted, ranked, or advertising behavior remains excluded from this mission and V1 scope.

## 10. Critical Audit (KILL CRITICAL)

| Risk | Problem | Impact | Prevention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform favoritism | The official profile could be treated as more important than normal businesses. | Loss of trust and unfair discovery. | Apply the same discovery eligibility rules to all entities. |
| Trust abuse | Platform ownership could self-assign high trust or ignore audit rules. | Credibility damage and governance conflict. | Use distinct Official Platform Verification and require auditability. |
| Ranking abuse | Official profile could be automatically placed first. | Discovery neutrality failure. | Explicitly prohibit automatic top ranking and hidden preference. |
| Advertising drift | Official profile could become a hidden promotion channel. | Scope creep into ads and paid visibility. | Exclude advertising, promoted placement, and paid visibility. |
| Marketplace drift | Services could be interpreted as sellable offerings. | Premature transactions, ordering, and payments. | Keep services informational until approved implementation governance. |
| Conflict of interest | Platform may compete with providers while controlling discovery. | Provider distrust and future legal/product risk. | Separate platform identity from discovery ranking and require neutrality rules. |
| Analytics abuse | Platform could use profile analytics to suppress competitors. | Privacy, fairness, and anti-competitive risk. | Permit only aggregated decision-support analytics and prohibit suppression. |
