## Repaired journeys and their contract boundaries

### Account, navigation and maps

Account return paths are confined to supported local routes, including driver selection and exact business/professional detail identifiers. Invalid, external or ambiguous returns do not become redirects. Existing native management and operational destinations retain their authority checks.

Category-to-nearby navigation preserves the selected city. Legacy aliases resolve to fixed local targets, including Taxi and delivery signup. The 27 SSR prefetch files do not explicitly forward arbitrary incoming queries; this report corrects the earlier unsupported claim that such queries were automatically carried. Continuity of a specific legacy query still requires a suitable redirect contract or acceptance evidence.

Map embeds load only after the user chooses to send the displayed location, and destination when present, to Google Maps. Consent resets on a changed location/destination. Coordinate bounds are checked, and an explicit external map link remains a user handoff. No actual GPS session, navigation route or live Maps/browser-permission acceptance follows from these fixtures.

### Owner businesses, products and advertisements

Native management uses backend-issued identifiers, exact domain fields and revision-bound updates. Business content submission is distinct from trust verification. Product submission respects required media and merchant eligibility. Public product images and private driver evidence use separate adapters and authority paths.

Classifieds content/media editing is confined to draft, inactive or rejected states; active and pending-review content stays locked. Integer revisions, target/media ownership and image size/type are bound to the request. Profile contact requires a linked owned business. These controls do not establish live ad approval, publication quota or payment acceptance.

Creation and recovery use a fresh captured-cookie server session plus a matching opaque actor key. A caller cannot substitute a raw user identity. Before dispatch, only an opaque request ID and canonical payload fingerprint are stored and verified; form values and image bytes are not persisted in the attempt ledger. If storage cannot preserve that guard, dispatch stops. Same-attempt recovery retains its original key. A later rejected replay does not erase an earlier unknown result. Old unscoped creation markers are quarantined instead of attributed to the current account.

Media and inbox access denial clears enclosing private editor state. Reads/writes are canceled or ignored after identity, record or component changes where applicable; a late result cannot be rendered as another profile's data. An uncertain non-idempotent media upload requires fresh evidence and explicit review before its local hold can be cleared. An unchanged list is not proof that the upload failed.

Consequently, the older source allowed individually valid inputs to compose an amount outside that storage range; a price of `999,999,999,999` multiplied by 2 already exceeds it. A stored maximum subtotal plus a delivery fee of `0.01` is another boundary.
