# V1 Scope

## Product Direction

Khedmah Digital V1 is an Arabic-first business growth platform foundation.

## In Scope for Foundation

- Repository governance documentation.
- Product scope documentation.
- Architecture direction documentation.
- Domain contract documentation.
- Operational definition of done documentation.
- Strategic vision documentation.

## Out of Scope for Foundation

- Backend implementation.
- Frontend implementation.
- APIs.
- Database models or migrations.
- Production infrastructure.
- New product features.

## Scope Protection

Reserved and future modules must remain documented only until explicitly approved.
